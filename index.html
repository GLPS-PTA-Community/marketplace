About the Community Marketplace

The Community Marketplace is a platform run by the GLPS PTA to help families give, sell, or buy school-related items like uniforms, books, toys, and sports equipment.

Email addresses from sellers are only shared when a buyer submits a contact request. It is then up to the seller to respond and manage all communication. The PTA is not involved in any contact beyond that point.

We do not take part in, manage, or profit from any financial transactions. Responsibility for all communication and exchanges lies with the individuals involved.

Listings are live for 30 days. You'll be reminded 7 days before expiry to renew. If not renewed or if marked as sold, listings will be removed from the site and the associated image will be permanently deleted.

Please use the site respectfully, and feel free to share your thoughts at: pta@greatlinfordprimaryschool.co.uk

— GLPS PTA Team
